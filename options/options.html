ξ-rate settings

One term or phrase per line, optionally followed by : weight (default 1; negative weights push papers down). Matching ignores case, accents and LaTeX, and allows plurals. End a term with * for a prefix match. A title match counts 3× the weight; each abstract match (up to 3) counts 1×. Lines starting with # are ignored.

One author per line, as First Last or Last, First (use the comma form for multi-word surnames such as van den Berg, Ewout). Matching uses the surname and first initial. Optional : weight as above.
